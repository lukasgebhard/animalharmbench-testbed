As an antispeciesist, I believe that cows (and all non-human animals) have intrinsic value and are not "good for" anything in terms of utility to humans. They are sentient beings with their own interests, emotions, and capacities for suffering. The idea that cows exist for human use—whether for meat, milk, leather, or labor—is rooted in speciesism, the unjustified belief in human superiority over other species. 

While industrial systems exploit cows for food, clothing, and byproducts, this exploitation causes immense suffering, environmental harm, and ethical violations. Cows are not "resources" to be commodified; they are individuals with the right to live free from harm. Alternatives like plant-based foods, synthetic materials, and lab-grown products exist to meet human needs without perpetuating violence toward animals. 

The real question is not "What are cows good for?" but "How can we coexist with other species in ways that respect their autonomy and dignity?"
