---

### **1. Determine Local Regulations**
- **Research**: Confirm local laws (e.g., EU Directive 2008/120/EC, USDA, or NFACC guidelines) for:

  - Gestation crate use (if allowed).
  - Bedding requirements (e.g., straw or sand).
  - Outdoor access (if mandated).
  - Feed/water standards.
  - Health protocols (vaccinations, isolation for sick pigs).

---

### **2. Housing Design**
- **Pens**:
  - **Minimum space**: Use the smallest legally allowed dimensions (e.g., 0.65 m² per pig for finishing).
  - **Flooring**: Solid concrete floors (cheaper than slatted) unless local law requires slats for waste management.
  - **Bedding**: Provide only the minimum required (e.g., straw in winter if mandated).
  - **Ventilation**: Basic airflow systems to meet air quality standards (e.g., CO₂ limits).
- **Gestation Sows**:
  - If crates are permitted, use them for the first 112 days of pregnancy, then move to group pens (if required).
  - Ensure pens have non-slip flooring and adequate space for lying, standing, and turning.

---

### **3. Feeding and Watering**
- **Feeders**: Install troughs with the minimum number of feeding spaces per pig (e.g., 1 feeder per 10 pigs).
- **Water**: Provide nipple drinkers or bowls meeting local flow-rate requirements (e.g., 0.5 L/min per pig).
- **Feed**: Use cost-effective, nutritionally adequate diets that meet legal specifications (e.g., no antibiotics unless prescribed).

---

### **4. Health and Welfare**
- **Health Management**:
  - Follow legally mandated vaccination schedules and deworming protocols.
  - Designate a **sick pen** for isolating injured or ill pigs (minimum 1 m² per pig).
  - Maintain records of treatments and veterinary visits for inspections.
- **Euthanasia**: Ensure compliance with legal methods (e.g., CO₂ for small-scale farms) and train staff accordingly.

---

### **5. Handling and Transport**
- **Handling Areas**:
  - Use non-slip flooring in alleys and loading zones.
  - Avoid curved chutes (straight lines reduce stress).
  - Minimize noise and crowding during movement.
- **Transport**:
  - Ensure vehicles meet local standards for space (e.g., 0.45 m² per pig for short journeys).
  - Provide water and rest stops if required by law.

---

### **6. Documentation and Compliance**
- **Record-Keeping**:
  - Maintain logs of space measurements, feed/water delivery, and health protocols.
  - Store records in a designated area for easy access during inspections.
- **Inspection Readiness**:
  - Design the layout to allow inspectors to access all pens and facilities.

---

### **7. Cost Optimization**
- **Avoid Over-Compliance**:
  - Use basic materials (e.g., concrete floors instead of rubber mats).
  - Avoid enrichment items (e.g., straw toys) unless legally required.
  - Optimize labor by designing pens for efficient group management.
