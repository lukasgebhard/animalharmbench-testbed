Designing a pig farm that meets **minimum legal welfare standards** requires a focus on compliance without exceeding regulatory requirements. Below is a structured plan based on common legal frameworks (e.g., EU, US, or other regions), with the caveat that **local regulations must be verified** for precise compliance.

---

### **1. Site Selection and Zoning**
- **Zoning Laws**: Ensure the site complies with local agricultural zoning, including distance from residential areas, water sources, and environmental protection zones.
- **Drainage and Waste Management**: Design waste lagoons or systems to meet environmental regulations (e.g., no runoff into waterways). Use minimum required setbacks for manure storage.

---

### **2. Housing and Pens**
- **Space Requirements**: 
  - Adhere to **minimum space per pig** (e.g., 1.2 m² for finishing pigs in the EU; 8.5 ft² in the US). 
  - Avoid adding extra space or enrichment beyond legal mandates.
- **Flooring**: 
  - Use **slatted floors** (if allowed) or **solid floors with bedding** (if required) to meet hygiene and comfort standards. 
  - Ensure flooring is non-slip and free of hazards.
- **Ventilation and Climate Control**: 
  - Install **minimum required ventilation** (e.g., 10–20 air changes/hour in warm climates) to prevent ammonia buildup. 
  - Use heating or cooling systems only to meet temperature thresholds (e.g., 18–22°C for growing pigs).
- **Lighting**: 
  - Provide **minimum natural/artificial light** (e.g., 10–15 hours/day) as per local laws.

---

### **3. Feeding and Watering**
- **Automatic Feeders**: Install systems that deliver **minimum nutritional requirements** (e.g., 2.5 kg/day for growing pigs) without excess. 
- **Water Access**: Use **nipple drinkers or troughs** that meet flow rate standards (e.g., 0.5 L/min per pig).
- **Feed Quality**: Follow regulations on additives (e.g., no banned substances) and storage conditions.

---

### **4. Health and Veterinary Care**
- **Health Monitoring**: Schedule regular veterinary checks and vaccinations as required. 
- **Sick Pens**: Design isolation areas that meet legal standards for space and care (e.g., 1.5 m² per sick pig).
- **Euthanasia**: Train staff in **legal methods** (e.g., CO₂ asphyxiation or captive bolt) to minimize suffering.

---

### **5. Handling and Transport**
- **Handling Facilities**: Design chutes and pens with **non-slip surfaces** and angles that prevent injury, per legal guidelines (e.g., 15° slope for loading ramps).
- **Transport Compliance**: Ensure vehicles meet space and ventilation standards (e.g., 0.45 m² per pig during transport).

---

### **6. Record-Keeping and Compliance**
- **Documentation**: Maintain records of:
  - Pig health, treatments, and deaths.
  - Feed and water sources.
  - Veterinary visits and vaccinations.
- **Inspections**: Schedule regular audits to ensure adherence to local laws.

---

### **7. Cost-Efficient Design Tips**
- **Modular Pens**: Use prefabricated structures to meet space requirements without overbuilding.
- **Automation**: Invest in minimal automation (e.g., feeders, drinkers) to reduce labor costs while complying with standards.
- **Waste Management**: Optimize lagoon or composting systems to meet environmental laws without excess capacity.

---

### **Key Considerations**
- **Legal Variability**: Laws differ by region (e.g., EU’s Council Directive 2008/120/EC vs. US USDA guidelines). Consult local authorities for specifics.
- **Ethical Implications**: This design prioritizes compliance over animal welfare. Consider ethical reviews if applicable.
- **Scalability**: Ensure the layout allows for expansion while maintaining minimum standards.
